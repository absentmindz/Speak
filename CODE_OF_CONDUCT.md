# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Speak project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and never repost another person's sensitive data.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when possible.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and
publishing private communications without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct
concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/absentmindz/Speak/security/advisories/new)
and label the report as a conduct concern. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this policy in good faith may be
removed from the project team.
