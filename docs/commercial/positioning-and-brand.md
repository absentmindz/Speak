# Positioning and preliminary brand check

## Primary positioning

> **Speak — private, local-first voice writing for Windows. Talk naturally and get polished text in any application.**

Use this message before listing individual models or technical integrations. The first demo should show the global shortcut, natural speech, a polished result, and delivery into another Windows application.

## Priority audiences

1. Developers and technical writers who dictate into VS Code, terminals, issue trackers, and AI tools.
2. Professionals who write email, reports, proposals, and support messages throughout the day.
3. People reducing keyboard use because of wrist pain, fatigue, or accessibility needs.
4. Privacy-conscious users who want local transcription when their hardware supports it.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Outcome:** write faster by speaking naturally.
2. **Trust:** local processing is available and cloud use is explicit.
3. **Workflow:** global hotkey, personal dictionary, corrections, history, and voice profile.
4. **Depth:** Whisper, Groq, LLM polishing, and local TTS are supporting details.

## Preliminary brand check

“Speak” is a common, descriptive English word and is crowded across speech, language, accessibility, and communications software. Treat the word mark as **not legally cleared**.

Launch guidance:

- Present the product consistently as **Speak for Windows** with the distinctive line **“Your voice, perfectly written.”**
- Do not claim exclusive ownership of the standalone word “Speak.”
- Keep the existing logo, repository identity, and `absentmindz/Speak` origin visible.
- Before paid advertising, trademark filing, or broad Store distribution, commission a professional clearance search covering exact and confusingly similar marks in relevant software/service classes.
- Reserve the Microsoft Store product name in Partner Center before finalizing MSIX identity fields.
- Check a future distinctive brand/domain before spending heavily on design, sponsorships, or legal filings.

Useful official starting points:

- USPTO trademark search: https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/
- Microsoft Partner Center name reservation and submission workflow: https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/apps/publish/publish-your-app/msix/create-app-submission

This is a practical product screen, not legal advice or a trademark opinion.
