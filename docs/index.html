03

Local when you choose local.

Speak makes the privacy boundary explicit. The engine and polishing options you configure determine what stays on your computer and what goes to a provider.

On your PC

Configured local workflow

With local Whisper configured, recorded audio is transcribed by a worker on the same computer. Speak can use its built-in formatter or a configured local LLM endpoint.

  • History, corrections, recordings, generated audio, logs, and crash reports may be stored in Speak’s local data directory.
  • No analytics or advertising telemetry service is configured in this repository.
  • Runtime and model setup may still require network downloads.
Only when configured

Optional online features

Cloud transcription uploads audio to your configured provider. Remote LLM polishing sends the transcript and local draft. Microsoft Edge TTS sends selected text to Microsoft’s online speech service.

  • Cloud features require deliberate configuration.
  • API keys are read from environment variables.
  • Each provider processes submitted data under its own terms and privacy policy.

05

Questions, answered plainly.

Does Speak keep my audio and text on my PC?

With local Whisper configured, recorded audio is transcribed on the same computer. Cloud transcription uploads audio to its provider; remote LLM polishing sends the transcript and local draft; Edge TTS sends selected text to Microsoft’s online speech service.

What does Speak require?

Windows 10 version 1809 or newer or Windows 11 on x64 hardware. Published releases are self-contained, while local Whisper and model-backed TTS require compatible runtimes, model weights, and related dependencies.

Can Speak write into every Windows app?

Speak is designed to copy or paste finished text into the active app. Automatic paste depends on the target app, permissions, and safety guards. You can copy the editable result manually when automatic paste is unavailable.

Is Speak free and open source?

Yes. Speak Community is available under Apache License 2.0, and the current Community release can be downloaded without charge. It remains pre-release software.

Can I buy the $21 supported version now?

No. The one-time supported launch is planned, but checkout is not live. The current link is only a public interest form; the paid scope and terms must be configured before payment opens.

Why does Windows show an unknown-publisher warning?

Speak’s installer is not currently Authenticode-signed. Verify it against the matching entry in SHA256SUMS.txt and expect Windows to show an unknown-publisher warning.
