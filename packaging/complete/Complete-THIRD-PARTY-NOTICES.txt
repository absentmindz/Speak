The baseline intentionally excludes CUDA, NVIDIA runtime libraries,
model-backed local TTS runtimes/models, large Whisper checkpoints, and
package-management tools. It includes only the small Edge TTS command runtime;
Microsoft neural voice synthesis still requires internet access.
