Speak's standard installer does not include an FFmpeg binary.

Local Whisper audio decoding requires a compatible FFmpeg installation supplied
separately by the user or environment. The separate Speak Complete Offline
Installer bundles its own pinned FFmpeg payload and includes the corresponding
license and provenance notices under packaging/complete/.

FFmpeg project and source:
  https://ffmpeg.org/
  https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git

Speak invokes FFmpeg as a separate executable and does not modify FFmpeg.
